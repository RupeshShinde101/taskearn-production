3.2 KYC Verification & Physical Onboarding Data (Sensitive Data)

  • Identity KYC: Selected document type (Aadhaar 12-digit, PAN 10-character, Voter ID, or Driving Licence), document number, and uploaded front/back document photos — used strictly for identity authentication, anti-fraud, task eligibility, and payout compliance.
  • Physical Onboarding (Sensitive Skills: Cleaning, Delivery, Driving, Cooking, Plumbing, Electrical, Carpentry, Moving & Packing, Pet Care, Babysitting, Elder Care): Full legal name, complete residential address, primary and alternative emergency phone numbers, optional experience/driving-licence notes, and admin physical visit verification status.

3.3 Tasks, Completion Proof, Wallet & Bank Withdrawal Details

  • Task Details: Task titles, descriptions, category/vehicle selections, Freelancer package/hourly options, pickup/drop/task addresses, saved locations, budgets, and platform fee ledgers.
  • Completion Proof Photos & Reviews: Camera/gallery proof photos submitted upon finishing a task, ratings, reviews, rank badges, and task release/abandonment counts.
  • Wallet Top-Ups: Processed by Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. We receive payment confirmation status, transaction reference IDs, and masked instrument summaries — we do NOT store card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs.
  • Bank Withdrawal Details: Bank Name, Account Holder Name, 9–18 digit Bank Account Number, and 11-character IFSC Code collected over HTTPS to execute KYC-verified NEFT/IMPS/Razorpay payouts. Optional "Save payment details" stores details locally on your device only.

3.4 In-App Encrypted Chat & Optional Call Coordination

  • Task Chat Messages & Images: Messages and photos exchanged in 1-on-1 task chat are transmitted over HTTPS/TLS with application-level task-room encryption (enc:v1:), and stored to deliver history and resolve safety or payment disputes.
  • External Dialer / Coordination Links: When active tasks surface a quick-dial ("Call") link, tapping it launches your device's telecom dialer or external app. Workmate4u does NOT record voice call audio; external carriers and apps operate under their own privacy policies.

3.5 Location, Notifications, Device & Technical Logs

  • GPS Location (Coarse, Fine & Background where permitted): Used with your permission to find nearby tasks (~10 km matching), place map pins, estimate route distance, and enable live active-task tracking. Background location runs only when you grant OS permission for active Helper tracking and stops upon completion or revocation.
  • Push Notifications: On mobile, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token and notification status for task alerts, chats, completions, and wallet updates. On web browsers, Workmate4u delivers updates in-app and via transactional email.
  • Device & Security Telemetry: Device model/browser user-agent, OS/app version, IP address, API error logs, and rate-limiting logs to prevent spam, fraud, and DNS/network failures.
  • Local Storage (localStorage, IndexedDB, Cache & App Preferences): Caches your signed JWT token, session state, theme choice, mode state, saved addresses, and optional saved payout fields on your device — see our Cookie Policy.

We process personal data to:

  • Verify your 18+ legal age, email OTP, KYC identity, and Sensitive Skill physical onboarding;
  • Create and maintain your Poster/Helper account and execute marketplace contracts;
  • Match tasks using location proximity, configured Helper skills, and automated AI ranking convenience (never profiling minors);

  • Fulfil statutory Indian legal duties (IT Act, Income Tax, GST, PMLA, RBI, and lawful court/police directions).
Lawful Basis (DPDP Act, 2023): Processing relies on your explicit informed consent (Section 6), contractual necessity for requested services, and statutory legitimate uses under Section 7 (compliance with Indian law, court orders, fraud prevention, and safety enforcement).

Your display name, avatar, rank, rating, and general area appear on tasks and offers. Once matched, necessary task address details, in-app encrypted chat, and (where enabled for active execution) phone coordination options are shared between the Poster and assigned Helper.

Each provider processes data only as instructed by us and under contractual and security commitments.

5.3 Legal, regulatory & safety disclosures

We may disclose records when strictly necessary to comply with lawful Indian court orders, police/cyber-crime requisitions under the IT Act or BNSS/BNS, tax/PMLA audits, or to investigate suspected child/adolescent exploitation, violence, or financial fraud.

Zero Data Brokering: Workmate4u does NOT sell personal data, share personal data with advertising networks, or run third-party ad-tech trackers on the Platform.

6. International Data Transfers

While our primary service targets India, certain cloud/messaging partners (such as Google/Firebase or SendGrid) may process data on servers outside India. All cross-border transfers comply with Section 16 of the DPDP Act, 2023 and applicable Government of India notifications.

7. Data Security & Breach Response

  • HTTPS / TLS encryption across all client-server API and Socket.IO traffic;
  • Passwords stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes;
  • Authentication uses signed JWT tokens stored on the user's device;
  • Task chat safeguarded via TLS + task-room application encryption (enc:v1:);
  • Restricted role-based access controls, audit logging, and automated rate-limiting on backend servers;
  • PCI-DSS compliant card/UPI processing solely on Razorpay's infrastructure.

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a personal data breach affecting you, we will notify affected Users and the Data Protection Board of India promptly as mandated under Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act, 2023.

9. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023 & Account Deletion

As a Data Principal in India, you have the right to:

  • Access Information (s. 11): Request a summary of personal data processed and third parties with whom it was shared;
  • Correction, Completion & Updating (s. 12): Update profile details in-app or request correction of inaccurate records;
  • Erasure / Withdraw Consent (s. 12 & s. 6): Delete your account via Profile → Delete My Account or email info@workmate4u.com. Deletion cannot complete while active tasks, open disputes, or negative wallet balances exist, and financial, KYC, tax, and fraud-prevention records required under Indian law are preserved strictly in restricted archive until statutory expiry;
  • Nominate (s. 14): Designate an adult nominee to exercise data rights in case of incapacity or death by emailing info@workmate4u.com;
  • Grievance Redressal & Appeal (s. 13): Contact our Grievance Officer first; unresolved grievances may be escalated to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Zero Tolerance for Children's & Minor Data (Under 18)

Under Section 2(f) and Section 9 of the DPDP Act, 2023, any person below eighteen (18) years of age is a child. Workmate4u does NOT target, onboard, profile, monitor, or knowingly process personal data of individuals under 18. Date-of-birth gates restrict registration strictly to 18+ adults. If we learn that an account belongs to or is operated by someone under 18, we immediately suspend access, terminate the account, and erase non-statutory personal data (retaining only minimum fraud/compliance block identifiers where legally required). Please report any suspected underage user immediately to info@workmate4u.com.
